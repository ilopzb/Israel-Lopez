# Tender Mercies

## Overview

Tender Mercies is a personal writing website where I share reflections on faith, family, life, books, and everyday experiences.

The goal is to create a peaceful and welcoming space where readers can slow down, reflect, and recognize God's hand in their own lives.

This project is also part of my journey learning front-end web development through BYU–Idaho.
