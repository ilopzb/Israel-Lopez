# Tender Mercies

## Project Purpose

Tender Mercies is a personal reflection blog designed to be a peaceful digital refuge.

The site shares thoughtful writing about faith, Jesus Christ, family, growth, repentance, hope, and recognizing the hand of God in ordinary life.

The goal is not simply to publish articles, but to create a reading experience that encourages visitors to slow down, reflect, and leave with a little more peace and hope.

## Brand and Design Direction

The visual identity is intentionally calm and natural.

Primary characteristics include:

* Soft sage, cream, and natural colors
* Generous whitespace
* Elegant serif typography
* Rounded cards and sections
* Gentle imagery
* Minimal distractions
* Comfortable long-form reading layouts

### Site Identity

**Name:** Tender Mercies

**Tagline:** Thoughts for quiet hearts

**Hero message:**
Come as you are. Leave a little lighter.

## Core Pages

### Home

The homepage introduces the Tender Mercies atmosphere and highlights recent reflections.

### Reflections

The Reflections archive provides a central place to browse published posts and future categories such as Faith, Life, and Family.

### About

The About page explains the purpose behind Tender Mercies and the personal story behind the project.

### Individual Reflections

Each reflection uses a focused long-form reading layout with:

* Category, date, and estimated reading time
* Scripture quotations
* Reflection questions
* Clear section headings
* A final weekly invitation
* A link back to the Reflections archive

## First Published Reflection

**What Am I Willing to Leave Behind?**

A reflection based primarily on Alma 22 and Alma 24, exploring conversion, mercy, covenants, and the symbolic meaning of the Anti-Nephi-Lehies burying their weapons.

The central invitation is to consider what attitudes, habits, influences, resentments, distractions, or other personal “weapons” may need to be left behind while following Jesus Christ.

## Publishing Goal

Tender Mercies should remain simple enough that new reflections can be published regularly without requiring major structural changes to the website.

The homepage will feature selected recent reflections while `blog.html` serves as the complete archive.

Future features may include newsletter subscriptions, functional category filtering, additional pages, and other improvements as the collection grows.
