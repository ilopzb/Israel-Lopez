About

Welcome. I'm glad you're here.

Hi, I'm Israel.

I have always believed that some of life's greatest lessons are found in quiet moments. Not necessarily during life's biggest milestones, but in the ordinary days when God gently reminds us that He is near.

Tender Mercies began as a simple desire to write those moments down—not because I have all the answers, but because I know what it feels like to need hope, direction, and peace.

Over time, I realized that many of the experiences that strengthened my faith were not dramatic miracles, but quiet reminders that the Lord had been walking beside me all along.

Why Tender Mercies?

The name comes from one of my favorite scriptural expressions: the tender mercies of the Lord. To me, they are the quiet evidence of God's love—often recognized only when we pause long enough to look back and see His hand in our lives.

Whether life's season is filled with joy or uncertainty, I believe God continues to speak through small and simple things. My hope is that these reflections encourage you to notice those moments in your own journey.
